# Sudoku – Play Like a Book privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

## Summary

Sudoku – Play Like a Book ("the app") has no accounts and does not ask for your email address or phone number. Your puzzles, progress and settings stay on your device. The app shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob, sends a few anonymous usage counts, and stores puzzle codes and group-game details you choose to share.

## What stays on your device

- Your puzzles, progress, notes, mistakes and hints.
- Your settings (theme, symbols, colours, fonts, timer, game mode).
- Volumes you have bought or downloaded.
- Photos you scan to import a puzzle. Text and QR reading happens on your device; the photo is not uploaded and is kept only in the app's temporary storage.

This data is deleted when you use "Reset all progress" (purchased volumes are kept), clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

## What is sent from the app

The app uses [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) to store the following. No user ID, device ID or account is attached to any of it.

- **Anonymous style counts.** When you pick a style or solve a puzzle, the app sends the style's name, whether it was picked or solved, the app version and the platform (for example "android"). We use this only to see which styles are popular.
- **Puzzle codes.** When you share, print or copy a puzzle's code, the code and the puzzle's starting numbers are stored so that anyone with the code can open the same puzzle.
- **Group games.** If you host or join a group game, the room's title, the puzzle, the name you type, and your correct and wrong answer counts are stored. Everyone in the room can see the names and scores. Rooms and their players are deleted automatically after about one day, and the host can remove a player at any time.
- **Volume downloads.** The app downloads the list of volumes and the volumes you own. Nothing about you is uploaded.

Like any internet request, these reveal your device's IP address to the server.

## Advertising

The app shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads, and in some regions to personalise them. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before any ad is loaded, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. See [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Ads are switched off once you own any paid volume.

## Purchases

Volumes are bought through Google Play (or the App Store). Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple; we never see your payment details. Your purchases are restored from the store, not from our servers.

## Other Google services

- **Fonts** are downloaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.
- **Install link.** On Android, the first time you open the app, it reads the Google Play install link once, only to open a puzzle code if the app was installed from a printed puzzle's link. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

## What we do not do

- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
- We do not collect your location, contacts or microphone.
- We do not sell your data.

## Who the app is for

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If the app starts collecting different data, this policy will be updated first and the store listing's data safety section will be changed to match.
